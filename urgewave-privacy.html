UrgeWave Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy describes how UrgeWave for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch handles your information. UrgeWave does not contain advertising, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

1. Information stored on your device

UrgeWave stores the following only on your device: your categories, session times and durations, optional intensity ratings, outcomes, triggers, body sensations, helpfulness answers, reflection notes, preferences, and any unfinished session so you can return to it.

There is no account, and we do not operate a server that receives this information. We cannot see, access or recover your records.

2. iPhone and Apple Watch

Categories can be sent from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch, and completed Watch sessions are sent to your iPhone, using Apple's WatchConnectivity service. A Watch entry stays queued on the Watch until your iPhone confirms it has been stored. Transfers may be delayed when your devices are not connected.

3. Device authentication, file protection and backups

You can choose to protect history and settings with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. This uses Apple's system authentication; UrgeWave never receives or stores biometric data.

Session files use iOS and watchOS file protection. Your device's own backups (such as iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include UrgeWave data, according to your device settings.

4. Exports and external links

If you export your history, the CSV file contains readable session details and notes. You choose where to send it, and the destination's handling of the file is outside our control. Exported copies are not removed when you delete history in the app.

The app links to external information websites, which have their own privacy practices.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries or all history from the app's History and Settings screens. Deletion applies to the device you delete it on: iPhone and Watch history are deleted separately. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. Backups and exported files must be managed separately.

6. Children's privacy

UrgeWave is intended for users aged 13 and over. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
