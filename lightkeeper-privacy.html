Lightkeeper · Support · Privacy · Terms

Privacy policy

This policy covers Lightkeeper and its widget, Live Activity and asset download extension. It describes the current release candidate. We do not operate a custom game server.

On your device

Puzzle progress, lantern placements, oil, settings and purchase delivery records are stored locally. A read-only beacon summary is shared with the widget through an on-device App Group. Local notifications are optional and scheduled on your device after permission. Live Activities display your puzzle status through Apple’s system.

Optional Apple services

If you connect iCloud, your complete save is stored through Apple’s GameSave/iCloud service under your Apple account. When saves differ, you choose which whole save to retain. Disconnecting does not delete the existing cloud save. Apple Game Center sign-in is optional; campaign stars, beacon scores and completed Duet counts may be submitted to Apple leaderboards when signed in. These services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy and account controls.

Apple processes purchases and subscriptions. Lightkeeper reads verified transaction and entitlement records to deliver purchases, prevent duplicate grants and restore eligible purchases. We do not receive your card details. Downloadable island content is intended to be delivered through Apple-hosted Background Assets.

Duet links

When you share a Duet link, it contains structured gameplay data: the puzzle seed, lantern placements, move count and elapsed play time. Share only with recipients you choose. The app does not provide a public feed, chat, player directory or developer-hosted upload service. Recipients and the messaging service you choose may retain the link.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The current build does not include or initialize the registered AppLovin MAX or TelemetryDeck SDKs. No advertising, analytics or attribution events are sent through those SDKs by the app, and the app does not request tracking permission. Any future activation will require an updated policy and appropriate disclosures and consent. We do not sell personal information.

This website and support

These static pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising scripts or forms. GitHub may process technical request information as hosting provider. If you email support, your email address, message and attachments are processed through the email provider to respond and resolve the request. Avoid sending passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. Support messages are retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; contact us to request deletion.

Your choices and deletion

No developer account registration is required. You can decline iCloud or Game Center, disable notifications in iOS Settings, and manage subscriptions in your Apple account. Removing the app removes its local app data, although Apple backups or synchronized data may remain. Use Apple’s iCloud storage controls to manage cloud data; contact support if you need help finding them. Purchase records held by Apple follow Apple’s retention rules. Other recipients’ copies of Duet links cannot be removed by us.

Audience and changes

Lightkeeper is a general-audience puzzle game and does not intentionally request children’s personal information. It is not represented as an App Store Kids Category app. This policy may change when features or services change; the update date appears below.
