A little light. A satisfying puzzle.

Lightkeeper is a native iPhone and iPad logic puzzle game about restoring light to a quiet coast. It is being prepared for release; this page does not announce App Store availability.

How to play

  1. Tap an open square to place a lantern. Tap again to remove it.
  2. Lanterns light open squares along their row and column until a wall stops the light.
  3. Light every open square. Two lanterns must never shine directly at each other.
  4. A numbered post needs exactly that many lanterns in the four squares immediately above, below, left and right. A 0 means none; a 1 means exactly one; a 2 means exactly two. Diagonals do not count.

A guided, interactive walkthrough introduces each rule before a small practice puzzle. Replay it from Settings → Replay guided walkthrough, or use the puzzle help button.

Your own pace

Explore Journey puzzles, try the daily beacon, exchange a Duet puzzle link with someone you choose, and collect keepsakes. Undo is free. Every puzzle can be solved without buying hints. Later puzzles introduce glass, mirrors and prisms.

Optional purchases

The game includes optional oil for hints, cosmetic themes, a season pass and a Keeper subscription. The purchase screen shows Apple’s localized real-money price before purchase. Product availability is subject to App Store configuration. No banners or interstitial ads are active.

Requires iOS 26 or later. The interface is currently in English.
