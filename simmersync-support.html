How Does Multi-Dish Sync Cook Work?

Open the Sync Cook tab, select your main dish and sides, and choose your target Serve Time. SimmerSync backward-schedules prep, oven preheat, active cooking, and resting times so every dish finishes hot together, and flags any oven temperature differences.
