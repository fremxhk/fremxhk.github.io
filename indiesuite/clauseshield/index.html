ClauseShield

ClauseShield helps individuals inspect agreements, understand flagged wording and prepare proposed changes for discussion.

  • Import an agreement as PDF, text or scanned pages and choose a role and category.
  • Review source quotes, screening flags and missing-protection checks; save or dismiss suggested replacements.
  • Compare paragraph versions and export a negotiation draft or redline comparison memo.

Screening and drafting assistance only. It is not legal advice or a completeness, enforceability or outcome guarantee. A qualified lawyer should assess the final agreement.
