VaultGlass

VaultGlass helps individuals keep receipts, item details, estimated warranty dates and home inventory in one place.

  • Import receipts from photos, document scans, PDFs or text. Review and correct extracted facts.
  • Track estimated return and warranty dates, attach evidence and prepare claim drafts.
  • Filter a home inventory by property and room, then export an insurance valuation PDF.

Coverage and depreciation are estimates. Always check merchant, manufacturer, card-provider and insurer terms.
