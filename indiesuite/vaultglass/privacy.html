VaultGlass Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current app implementation. No developer-operated account, advertising service, third-party analytics SDK or external AI API is used.

Your documents and records

Imported documents, extracted values, saved records and generated drafts are processed and stored on your device. Apple Vision and, when available, Apple Foundation Models perform on-device processing. Documents are not uploaded to a developer server or external AI provider.

Optional iCloud synchronization

When iCloud is available and configured, records can synchronize through an app-specific private CloudKit container associated with your Apple Account. Otherwise the app uses local storage. Apple operates iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy. Local and cloud stores are separate; use export/import when transferring between them.

Purchases and diagnostics

Apple handles payment, subscription management and purchase restoration. The app reads verified transaction entitlements and stores a local usage counter in Keychain and app preferences. It does not receive payment-card details. Local QA telemetry measures processing duration and memory; MetricKit uses Apple’s system diagnostics framework. No analytics are transmitted to a developer endpoint.

Permissions, exports and deletion

Camera and photo access are requested for document capture or selection. Selected files are accessed for import. You control exports and sharing; plain archives contain readable data, while password-protected archives use encryption. Keep backup destinations and passwords secure. Remove records using available app controls or remove the app’s local data through iOS Settings. Manage synchronized data in your Apple iCloud storage settings. Removing a local app copy does not necessarily delete synchronized data or exports. Uninstalling does not guarantee clearing the Keychain usage counter.

Contact and changes

For privacy requests, email fremxhk@gmail.com. Do not include full lab reports, contracts or payment information in an initial email. Material policy changes will be reflected on this page.
