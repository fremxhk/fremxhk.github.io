# BioPulse Local — App Review information

Status: prepared draft. Do not submit until the physical-device evidence below is complete. This is not a claim that hardware QA has been performed.

1. **Physical-device demonstration:** Pending. Record on a physical iPhone/iPad running the latest available operating system. Begin with launching the installed Release/TestFlight build, demonstrate import, review, saved history, export, and the Pro purchase/restore path. Provide device model, OS version, build number and the recording URL. The simulator captures in this repository are UI evidence only.
2. **Purpose and audience:** BioPulse Local helps adults organize lab reports, compare saved biomarker measurements and prepare questions for clinician visits. Intended audience: Adults who want a personal lab history and educational visit-preparation tools. This is a general-public app, not a private employee or organization distribution.
3. **Access and setup:** No registration, login or demo credentials are required. Launch the app, use the document import action and select “Try a sample document” to generate a synthetic example locally. No external sample file is needed. Use Import Labs, verify the source readings, then open Correlation Studio and Doctor Brief. Five completed AI actions are available free; open Pro to purchase or restore additional access. The floating QA seeder and entitlement overrides exist only in Debug and are not part of the Release app.
4. **External services:** Apple StoreKit/App Store for purchases and restore; Apple CloudKit/iCloud for optional private synchronization; Apple Vision and Foundation Models frameworks for on-device OCR and generation; Apple SwiftData, PDFKit, PhotosPicker/document camera, App Intents and MetricKit platform frameworks. Apple HealthKit is read-only and optional; synthetic demo health trends are labeled. There is no developer-operated backend, ad SDK, third-party analytics endpoint, or third-party AI API. No Private Cloud Compute route is invoked by this implementation.
5. **Regions:** The app’s intended tools and content are consistent across regions, with an English interface. Apple model availability depends on supported device, language, region and OS settings; deterministic on-device extraction is available when the model is unavailable. Apple determines purchase availability, price, tax and trial eligibility. iCloud and HealthKit depend on account, permission and platform availability. There is no region-specific regulated service.
6. **Rights and regulated activity:** Educational organization and estimates only. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease. Reference intervals are illustrative, not individualized clinical thresholds. Follow your clinician and laboratory instructions. PhenoAge is an estimate, not a clinical assessment. No professional credential, regulatory authorization or third-party content license is claimed. Synthetic QA documents are generated locally; users import their own files. If Apple requests additional substantiation or professional review, obtain it before submission. There are no accounts or publicly shared user-content feeds, so account deletion and public-content reporting/blocking flows do not apply.
