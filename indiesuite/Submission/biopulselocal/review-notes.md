# BioPulse Local — App Review information

Review package: version 1.0.0. No app registration or login is required.

1. **Demonstration:** No app account is required. Use the built-in synthetic example from the import flow, review its original source and saved result, then explore the available exports. Sample documents are generated locally and use the same review screens as user-provided content.
2. **Purpose and audience:** BioPulse Local helps adults organize lab reports, compare saved biomarker measurements and prepare questions for clinician visits. Intended audience: Adults who want a personal lab history and educational visit-preparation tools. This is a general-public app, not a private employee or organization distribution.
3. **Access and setup:** No registration, login or demo credentials are required. Launch the app, use the document import action and select “Try a sample document” to generate a synthetic example locally. No external sample file is needed. Use Import Labs, verify the source readings, then open Correlation Studio and Doctor Brief. Five completed AI actions are available free; open Pro for paid additional access.
4. **External services:** Apple StoreKit/App Store for purchases; on-device health storage; Apple Vision and Foundation Models frameworks for on-device OCR and generation; Apple SwiftData, PDFKit, PhotosPicker/document camera, App Intents and MetricKit platform frameworks. Apple HealthKit is read-only and optional; synthetic demo health trends are labeled. There is no developer-operated backend, ad SDK, third-party analytics endpoint, or third-party AI API. No Private Cloud Compute route is invoked by this implementation.
5. **Regions:** Paid-product availability is USA only in the current configuration.  The app’s intended tools and content are consistent across regions, with an English interface. Apple model availability depends on supported device, language, region and OS settings; deterministic on-device extraction is available when the model is unavailable. Apple determines purchase availability, price, tax and trial eligibility. Optional HealthKit access depends on user permission and platform availability. There is no region-specific regulated service.
6. **Rights and regulated activity:** Educational organization and estimates only. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease. Reference intervals are illustrative, not individualized clinical thresholds. Follow your clinician and laboratory instructions. PhenoAge is an estimate, not a clinical assessment. No professional credential, regulatory authorization or third-party content license is claimed. Synthetic QA documents are generated locally; users import their own files.  There are no accounts or publicly shared user-content feeds, so account deletion and public-content reporting/blocking flows do not apply.

Health records use an on-device SwiftData store, with no automatic CloudKit mirroring or iCloud/push entitlement. Health-data directories request exclusion from system backup. Complete health archives require a user-chosen passphrase of at least 12 characters; users explicitly choose any export destination.
