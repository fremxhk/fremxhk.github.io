# EchoBrief — App Review information

Preparation for version 1.0.0. No developer registration or login is required.

1. **Demonstration:** No app account is required. Use the built-in synthetic example from the main capture or creation flow, then review the saved result and available exports. The example uses the same review screens as user-provided content.
2. **Purpose and audience:** Turn a meeting transcript into a local brief and source-linked commitments you can verify. Intended audience: Individuals and small teams keeping personal meeting records and follow-up tasks. General-public distribution, not a private employee-only app.
3. **Access and setup:** Choose New meeting brief, then Use a synthetic example and Create brief. Open the saved meeting, inspect a commitment and its source, verify owner/task/date and explicitly confirm before adding a Reminder or Calendar event. Export meeting brief generates the PDF. Text examples work without microphone or calendar permission. Five completed AI actions are free; Pro enables additional actions. No external sample file or login credentials are required.
4. **External services:** Apple StoreKit/App Store for purchases; optional private CloudKit storage; SwiftData, PDFKit, Vision and Foundation Models for supported local data, OCR and generation. Apple AVFoundation and Speech for consented recording and on-device transcription. Apple EventKit for explicitly confirmed Reminders/Calendar writes. No developer-operated backend, third-party AI API, external tax/health/VIN/price data service, ad SDK or developer analytics endpoint is used.
5. **Regions:** English interface. The app and paid products are configured for USA availability for this submission. Apple determines purchase/trial eligibility and supported on-device model and speech language/region availability. iCloud and system permissions depend on the user account and platform. No separate regional service is supplied.
6. **Rights and regulated activity:** Obtain participant consent before recording. Speech input uses the English (U.S.) recognizer and requires supported on-device recognition; pasted text remains available when speech is unavailable. Relative dates remain unset until verified. Cost estimates depend on the rates and attendance you enter. The app does not message meeting participants. Users provide their own material; synthetic examples are generated locally. No regulated professional service, professional credential, certification or protected third-party content license is claimed. There are no app accounts or public user-content feeds.
