# ClauseShield — App Review information

Review package: version 1.0.0. No app registration or login is required.

1. **Demonstration:** No app account is required. Use the built-in synthetic example from the import flow, review its original source and saved result, then explore the available exports. Sample documents are generated locally and use the same review screens as user-provided content.
2. **Purpose and audience:** ClauseShield helps individuals inspect agreements, understand flagged wording and prepare proposed changes for discussion. Intended audience: Freelancers, creators, tenants and individuals preparing to discuss an agreement. This is a general-public app, not a private employee or organization distribution.
3. **Access and setup:** No registration, login or demo credentials are required. Launch the app, use the document import action and select “Try a sample document” to generate a synthetic example locally. No external sample file is needed. Choose a role/category, import the sample, review each clause and open Pushback to prepare a draft. Five completed AI actions are available free; open Pro for paid additional access.
4. **External services:** Apple StoreKit/App Store for purchases; Apple CloudKit/iCloud for optional private synchronization; Apple Vision and Foundation Models frameworks for on-device OCR and generation; Apple SwiftData, PDFKit, PhotosPicker/document camera, App Intents and MetricKit platform frameworks. No external legal database or remote legal AI service is used. There is no developer-operated backend, ad SDK, third-party analytics endpoint, or third-party AI API. No Private Cloud Compute route is invoked by this implementation.
5. **Regions:** Paid-product availability is USA only in the current configuration. The app’s intended tools and content are consistent across regions, with an English interface. Apple model availability depends on supported device, language, region and OS settings; deterministic on-device extraction is available when the model is unavailable. Apple determines purchase availability, price, tax and trial eligibility. iCloud synchronization depends on account, permission and platform availability. There is no region-specific regulated service.
6. **Rights and regulated activity:** Screening and drafting assistance only. It is not legal advice or a completeness, enforceability or outcome guarantee. A qualified lawyer should assess the final agreement. No professional credential, regulatory authorization or third-party content license is claimed. Synthetic QA documents are generated locally; users import their own files.  There are no accounts or publicly shared user-content feeds, so account deletion and public-content reporting/blocking flows do not apply.
