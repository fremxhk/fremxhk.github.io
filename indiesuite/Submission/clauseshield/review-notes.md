# ClauseShield — App Review information

Status: prepared, unsubmitted draft. Physical iPhone QA is documented in the version-qualified test inventory; final video publication and remaining submission declarations must be complete before submission.

1. **Physical-device demonstration:** Physical iPhone 18 Pro Max, iOS 27.2 beta (24B115), TestFlight version 1.0.0 build 2. Installed-app tests exercise contract import, risk review, saved replacement decisions, negotiation draft and the comparison PDF. Pro access and paid-feature recordings are undergoing final verification. The final recording URL will be inserted after importing and reviewing the native physical-device clip. See the physical test inventory for exact evidence and remaining platform coverage; simulator captures do not substitute for physical recordings.
2. **Purpose and audience:** ClauseShield helps individuals inspect agreements, understand flagged wording and prepare proposed changes for discussion. Intended audience: Freelancers, creators, tenants and individuals preparing to discuss an agreement. This is a general-public app, not a private employee or organization distribution.
3. **Access and setup:** No registration, login or demo credentials are required. Launch the app, use the document import action and select “Try a sample document” to generate a synthetic example locally. No external sample file is needed. Choose a role/category, import the sample, review each clause and open Pushback to prepare a draft. Five completed AI actions are available free; open Pro for paid additional access. The floating QA seeder and entitlement overrides exist only in Debug and are not part of the Release app.
4. **External services:** Apple StoreKit/App Store for purchases; Apple CloudKit/iCloud for optional private synchronization; Apple Vision and Foundation Models frameworks for on-device OCR and generation; Apple SwiftData, PDFKit, PhotosPicker/document camera, App Intents and MetricKit platform frameworks. No external legal database or remote legal AI service is used. There is no developer-operated backend, ad SDK, third-party analytics endpoint, or third-party AI API. No Private Cloud Compute route is invoked by this implementation.
5. **Regions:** Current draft paid-product availability is USA only, pending the owner’s rollout decision.  The app’s intended tools and content are consistent across regions, with an English interface. Apple model availability depends on supported device, language, region and OS settings; deterministic on-device extraction is available when the model is unavailable. Apple determines purchase availability, price, tax and trial eligibility. iCloud and HealthKit depend on account, permission and platform availability. There is no region-specific regulated service.
6. **Rights and regulated activity:** Screening and drafting assistance only. It is not legal advice or a completeness, enforceability or outcome guarantee. A qualified lawyer should assess the final agreement. No professional credential, regulatory authorization or third-party content license is claimed. Synthetic QA documents are generated locally; users import their own files. If Apple requests additional substantiation or professional review, obtain it before submission. There are no accounts or publicly shared user-content feeds, so account deletion and public-content reporting/blocking flows do not apply.
