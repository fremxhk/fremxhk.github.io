# DeductGlass — App Review information

Preparation for version 1.0.0. No developer registration or login is required.

1. **Demonstration:** No app account is required. Use the built-in synthetic example from the main capture or creation flow, then review the saved result and available exports. The example uses the same review screens as user-provided content.
2. **Purpose and audience:** Organize business-expense evidence and compare illustrative tax methods before professional review. Intended audience: Self-employed adults and small-business owners organizing their own records. General-public distribution, not a private employee-only app.
3. **Access and setup:** Choose Add receipt or CSV, then Try a sample document. Open the resulting expense, check its facts and use Verify evidence. Open Tax profile & method comparison, review the selected year/framework and export the CPA evidence binder or separate Singapore records. Five completed AI actions are free; Pro enables additional actions. No external sample file or login credentials are required.
4. **External services:** Apple StoreKit/App Store for purchases; optional private CloudKit storage; SwiftData, PDFKit, Vision and Foundation Models for supported local data, OCR and generation. No developer-operated backend, third-party AI API, external tax/health/VIN/price data service, ad SDK or developer analytics endpoint is used.
5. **Regions:** English interface. The app and paid products are configured for USA availability for this submission. Apple determines purchase/trial eligibility and supported on-device model and speech language/region availability. iCloud and system permissions depend on the user account and platform. US tax-method estimates and Singapore recordkeeping are explicitly separated; other jurisdictions are not represented as covered.
6. **Rights and regulated activity:** Estimates are not tax advice, a filing service or a deduction guarantee. Eligibility, income limits, carryovers and local rules require qualified review. Stored amounts are USD; no currency conversion is performed. Users provide their own material; synthetic examples are generated locally. No regulated professional service, professional credential, certification or protected third-party content license is claimed. There are no app accounts or public user-content feeds.
