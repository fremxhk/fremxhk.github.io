BioPulse Local Support

For help, email fremxhk@gmail.com. Include the app version, device model, OS version and a description of the issue. Redact personal information from screenshots.

Getting started

Use the app’s import action and choose “Try a sample document” to explore the main flow. No login or external sample file is needed.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases on the Pro screen with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Manage subscriptions in the App Store or iOS Settings. Each app has its own Pro products.

Data

Use Backup & Privacy to export/import portable archives. Keep encrypted archive passwords safe; password recovery is not available. Verify extracted values against the original document.

Scope

Educational organization and estimates only. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease. Reference intervals are illustrative, not individualized clinical thresholds. Follow your clinician and laboratory instructions. PhenoAge is an estimate, not a clinical assessment.
