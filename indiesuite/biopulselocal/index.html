BioPulse Local

BioPulse Local helps adults organize lab reports, compare saved biomarker measurements and prepare questions for clinician visits.

  • Import a lab PDF or document photo and verify the extracted values and units.
  • Compare biomarker history with optional read-only Apple Health trends and dated lifestyle notes.
  • Prepare clinician questions, export a doctor brief and record laboratory preparation tasks.

Educational organization and estimates only. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent disease. Reference intervals are illustrative, not individualized clinical thresholds. Follow your clinician and laboratory instructions. PhenoAge is an estimate, not a clinical assessment.
