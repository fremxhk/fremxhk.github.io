BioPulse Local Privacy Policy

This policy describes the consumer release candidate under validation, updated 19 November 2026. No developer-operated account, advertising service, third-party analytics SDK or external AI API is used.

Your documents and records

Imported documents, extracted values, saved records and generated drafts are processed and stored on your device. Apple Vision and, when available, Apple Foundation Models perform on-device processing. Documents are not uploaded to a developer server or external AI provider.

On-device health storage

Lab reports and saved health records stay in this app’s on-device store. This candidate does not automatically synchronize health records through CloudKit. Its data directories request exclusion from system backups; the operating system controls backup and migration behavior. Keep your own password-protected archive before deleting the app or changing devices.

Earlier betas used a private iCloud container. Existing cached records are retained when opening the new local store. This change does not download cloud-only records or automatically delete earlier remote copies. Manage earlier cloud data separately through Apple’s controls.

Purchases and diagnostics

Apple handles payment, subscription management and purchase restoration. The app reads verified transaction entitlements and stores a local usage counter in Keychain and app preferences. It does not receive payment-card details. Local QA telemetry measures processing duration and memory; MetricKit uses Apple’s system diagnostics framework. No analytics are transmitted to a developer endpoint.

Health data

Optional, read-only HealthKit permission covers resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep, exercise and VO2 max. Permission can be denied or revoked in Apple Health/Settings. Health data is used for local trend display and is not sold, used for advertising, or sent to a developer server. Raw HealthKit samples are not included in the app’s portable archives or persisted by the app to CloudKit. Synthetic trend data is clearly labeled.

Permissions, exports and deletion

Camera and photo access are requested for document capture or selection. Selected files are accessed for import. You control exports and sharing; complete health archive exports require a passphrase of at least 12 characters. Legacy plain archives can be imported locally. Reports and other explicitly shared exports may contain readable sensitive data; choose trusted destinations. Keep backup destinations and passwords secure. Remove records using available app controls or remove the app’s local data through iOS Settings. Manage synchronized data in your Apple iCloud storage settings. Removing a local app copy does not necessarily delete synchronized data or exports. Uninstalling does not guarantee clearing the Keychain usage counter.

Contact and changes

For privacy requests, email fremxhk@gmail.com. Do not include full lab reports, contracts or payment information in an initial email. Material policy changes will be reflected on this page.
