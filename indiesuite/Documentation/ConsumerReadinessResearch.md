# Consumer completeness and launch pricing

Research and implementation review: 19 November 2026. Covers all eight apps. This is a release work plan and evidence ledger, not a declaration that unfinished candidates have passed QA.

## Findings from primary sources

| Consumer need | Evidence | Decision for this suite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find an item after the library grows; take records elsewhere | [Itemtopia](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/itemtopia-inventory/id952405279) describes item/location search, receipt storage, reminders and PDF/CSV exports. Its US listing showed $7.99 monthly and $79.99 yearly. | Add local multiword, case/accent-insensitive library search to all eight apps. Keep backups and access to existing records outside the AI-action purchase gate. |
| Review and correct extracted facts before relying on them | [ClausePocket](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/clausepocket-contract-review/id6822130646) describes source preview, contract review and sharing; its listing showed $7.99 monthly, $19.99 yearly and $39.99 lifetime, plus a $5.99 base app. [Lab Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lab-tracker/id6444037998) emphasizes lab entry/history, while a customer asks for missing marker support. | Preserve explicit coverage limits; finish BioPulse source-value correction with supported-unit validation, recalculation and removal of superseded summaries. Do not claim support for every possible laboratory marker or legal jurisdiction. |
| Avoid losing a draft or journal entry | A [Cara Care customer review](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cara-care-ibs-fodmap-tracker/id1133687886) describes a report disappearing after a dismissal gesture. This is one qualitative example, not a measured complaint rate. | Require explicit discard for unfinished source, meeting and card drafts; block accidental swipe dismissal. Provide food-label correction, independent repeated meals and symptom-entry removal. |
| Own useful records over the long term | [AnkiMobile](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ankimobile-flashcards/id373493387) listed $24.99 once, with synchronization and a mature review workflow. [Just Press Record by Open Planet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/just-press-record/id1033342465) listed $6.99 once, with recording, transcription and sync. | Show lifetime before subscriptions. Back up Recall scheduling and cue-card settings, meeting source-linked tasks, food observations, repair decisions and receipt attachments. Avoid promising a cross-app license that StoreKit does not provide. |
| Affordable car recordkeeping and useful comparisons | [Simply Auto](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-auto-mileage-tracker/id893278325) advertises service/expense records, reminders, exports and selectable units. Its US IAP listing included $9.99 Gold and $29.99 one-time Platinum. | Keep Torque focused on estimate review and explicit lease assumptions; add searchable reviews and portable vehicle/estimate records. Do not present a VIN checksum as a history lookup or an unsupported price estimate as market data. |
| Clear import/export and accessibility | [Genius Scan](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/scanner-app-genius-scan/id377672876) describes document search, exports and on-device processing and declares multiple accessibility features. | Exercise native layouts, readable labels, error states, import boundaries and actual exports. Do not copy another app's accessibility declarations without verifying our own. |

Listings are snapshots, not a like-for-like feature benchmark or proof of market share. IAP lists may contain legacy offers and do not establish their billing period unless separately labeled. No review scraping statistics or invented user-study sample is used.

## Selected additions and acceptance criteria

| App | Addition | Required completed behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All eight | Local library search; clearer lifetime-first purchase presentation | Match all search words across relevant saved fields; ignore case and accents; blank search shows the library; no-results state; correct filtered deletion mapping; native localized purchase prices; clear per-app license and five total free successful AI actions. |
| DeductGlass | Complete backup, confirmed deletion, selected-year spreadsheet export | Preserve receipt bytes, tax profile, business-purpose/verification fields and IDs. Export only the chosen tax year with currency, category and verification. Preserve new transaction calendar dates independently of device time zone; earlier records offer an explicit date-confirmation step. Quote CSV cells and neutralize spreadsheet formulas; re-import quoted/reordered headers and LF/CRLF/CR safely, rejecting unsupported currency before any save. Imported CSV rows require a new verification; complete backups preserve the original verified status. |
| EchoBrief | Complete backup, confirmed deletion, draft protection and editable briefs | Preserve transcripts, commitments, source quotes, priorities, dates and dispatch references; canceled edits remain in the composer until explicitly discarded. Correct title/summary without changing the original transcript or source-linked tasks. No participant messages are sent. |
| NutriLoupe | Complete backup, corrections, repeat meals, symptom deletion, CSV journal | Recalculate local ingredient matches after a correction, preserve linked observations, reject impossible timestamps, and give a repeated meal a new ID/time with no inherited symptoms. Export meals with and without linked symptoms. |
| TorqueShield | Complete backup, quote corrections and confirmed deletion | Preserve vehicle assumptions, source estimates, every line item, decisions and optional benchmark/source without inventing prices. |
| RecallGlass | Complete backup, manual/editable study cards, confirmed library deletion, draft protection | Preserve deck/card relationships, review schedule/history and full speech/cue-card content, layout and timing settings. Create manual cards without AI; preserve review history unless explicitly reset. |
| BioPulse Local | Correct extracted lab values; searchable/deletable panel history | Validate finite, nonnegative numbers and supported units before mutation; normalize consistently; recompute affected saved estimates, including later panels that reuse history; clear summaries based on earlier values. Invalid/canceled edits leave saved facts unchanged. |
| VaultGlass / ClauseShield | Search existing inventory and contract history | Search receipt/merchant/serial/location and contract/counterparty/source text while retaining existing archive recovery, exports and saved review decisions. Confirm contract deletion. Edit proposed replacement wording before accepting/exporting it while retaining the original quote and saved decision. |

The five new backup formats use typed versioned snapshots of all 13 existing model types (122 scalar fields), including linked children and receipt bytes. Imports are additive and atomic, reject incompatible app/version, duplicate archive identifiers and dangling parent references, and keep existing edited identifiers. Existing preferences win by default; an explicit import option restores supported preference keys without granting purchase access. Backup files have an explicit 100 MB bound; export must never report success for a file that import would reject. Encryption uses the existing authenticated, password-based archive implementation; losing the password is not recoverable.

## Pricing decision

Free download for every app. US launch prices saved and read back in App Store Connect are **$0.99/month, $5.99/year, $14.99 lifetime per app**, replacing $4.99/$29.99/$79.99. These are reductions of approximately 80%, 80% and 81%. The annual plan retains its eligible one-week trial; no fabricated sale countdown or cross-suite entitlement is added. Prices shown to customers come from StoreKit. Availability remains USA-only unless a separate distribution decision is made; equalized prices alone do not enable another territory.

The absence of a developer-hosted inference service reduces variable infrastructure costs; it does not produce a 100% net margin. Apple's [subscription documentation](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/) and [Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/) describe commissions and eligibility. Program enrollment has not been assumed. At 30%/15% commission, before taxes, refunds, currency adjustments and maintenance, $14.99 yields approximately $10.49/$12.74; $5.99 yields $4.19/$5.09; $0.99 yields $0.69/$0.84. These are illustrations, not an account revenue forecast. Low introductory pricing should be evaluated against conversion, refunds and support effort after launch; no analytics collection is being introduced.

## Release gate

Do not call these additions submission-ready until their concrete tests, full relevant regression suites, native phone/tablet layouts, new signed candidates, App Store catalog prices and metadata, current screenshots and GitHub documentation have been verified. Automatic production CloudKit mirroring is a separate gate from direct CloudKit service tests for the six non-health apps. BioPulse Local and NutriLoupe instead require on-device storage and migration/backup validation; see [health-storage policy](HealthStoragePolicy.md). Earlier passing builds remain historical evidence, not proof that these new changes pass. No finite test set can guarantee zero customer complaints; the release decision is based on verified advertised workflows, recovery paths and clear supported limits.
