NutriLoupe privacy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. This describes the current pre-release implementation.

Your records

Document reading, calculations and supported Apple Intelligence analysis run on your device. Records are stored locally, or in your private iCloud container when that storage is available. The developer does not receive your documents, journal entries or generated content.

Apple services and exports

Apple handles purchases and may process account and diagnostic information under its own policies. Debug QA diagnostics remain local. There is no developer-operated analytics, advertising or external AI server. Sharing and printing send the selected export to the destination or printer you choose; protect sensitive exports.

Control

You can remove saved records in the app and control permissions in iOS Settings. Copies you export, system reminders/events and backups require separate management. There is no app account to register or delete.
