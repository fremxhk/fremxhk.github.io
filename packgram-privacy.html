PackGram Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

PackGram is built on an offline-first, on-device architecture. We do not operate developer servers that collect, track, or sell your trips, gear weights, or packing lists. PackGram contains zero ads and zero third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

1. On-Device Storage & Apple Intelligence

  • Your Gear Closet, trips, item weights, and compliance checks are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple SwiftData.
  • On eligible devices with Apple Intelligence enabled, PackGram's Trip Architect uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework (`SystemLanguageModel.default`) locally on your device. Trip prompts are never transmitted to third-party AI APIs.

2. Exports & Purchases

CSV and PDF packing manifests are generated locally and shared only when you initiate a share via the iOS Share Sheet. The optional $2.99 one-time PackGram Lifetime Pro purchase is processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2.

3. Contact

For privacy questions or support, email fremxhk@gmail.com.
