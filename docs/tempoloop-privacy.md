# TempoLoop Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 2026

TempoLoop is built on an offline-first, on-device architecture. We do not operate developer servers that collect, track, or sell your repertoire pieces, practice sessions, tempo targets, or lesson notes. TempoLoop contains zero ads, zero user accounts, and zero third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

## 1. On-Device Storage & Apple Intelligence
- Your repertoire pieces, bar-range passages, spaced-repetition mastery scores, and practice logs are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple SwiftData and local UserDefaults.
- On eligible devices with Apple Intelligence enabled, TempoLoop's Session Architect uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework (`SystemLanguageModel.default`) locally on your device. Practice prompts are never transmitted to third-party AI servers.

## 2. Audio Engine, Exports & Purchases
TempoLoop's metronome synthesizes audio locally in real time without recording from your microphone. Teacher Lesson PDF and CSV reports are created locally on your device and shared only when you initiate a share via the iOS Share Sheet. The optional `$4.99` one-time TempoLoop Lifetime Pro purchase is processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2.
