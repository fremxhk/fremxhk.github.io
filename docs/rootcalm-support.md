## How Does Check-First ("Still Damp -> Snooze +2d") Work?
Instead of pushing blind watering schedules that cause root rot, RootCalm reminds you to touch the top 2 inches of soil. Logging **Still Damp** postpones care by 2 days and lengthens that plant's learned interval automatically.
