Support & playing guide

Contact fremxhk@gmail.com for Lightkeeper support. Include the app version, device model, iOS version, puzzle or date, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful; please remove private information.

What do 0, 1 and 2 mean?

Each numbered post counts lanterns immediately above, below, left and right. A 0 forbids adjacent lanterns. A 1 requires exactly one. A 2 requires exactly two. Diagonal squares never count.

Getting started

Launch the app and follow the interactive walkthrough, then open Journey and choose the first island. Tap open squares to toggle lanterns. Light all open squares, satisfy every numbered post, and ensure no two lanterns shine at each other. Use Undo to reverse a move or the question-mark button for help. Settings → Replay guided walkthrough restarts the demonstration.

Daily beacon and Duet

Open Coast for the daily beacon. The day follows your device’s local calendar and time zone. In Duet, create or open a puzzle link and share it using the system share sheet. No developer account or login is required. Only share with people you choose.

Purchases and subscriptions

Open Harbor to see available purchases and Apple’s localized prices. Use Restore Purchases for eligible non-consumable purchases and subscriptions. Consumable oil is not restored as a new purchase; keep your save safe. To manage or cancel a subscription, use Apple subscription settings. Refund requests are handled by Apple.

Save and download issues

Basic bundled puzzles work offline. Optional iCloud sync, Game Center, purchases and additional island downloads need the relevant Apple services and network access. When iCloud saves differ, choose the complete save you want to retain. Disconnecting iCloud does not delete the stored cloud copy. If content is unavailable, check your connection and retry; do not delete the app to troubleshoot before safeguarding your progress.
