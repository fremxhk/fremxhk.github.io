FrameSun Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

FrameSun is built on an offline-first, 100% on-device architecture. We do not operate developer servers, user accounts, advertising networks, or third-party analytics SDKs. Your film roll logs, frame exposure notes, lens bags, and saved solar scout locations remain on your device.

1. On-Device Data & Apple Intelligence

  • Film rolls, frame shutter/aperture logs, reciprocity calculations, and custom shooting locations are stored locally in Apple SwiftData and UserDefaults on your device.
  • On eligible devices with Apple Intelligence enabled, FrameSun's Lighting & Emulsion Scout uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework (SystemLanguageModel.default) locally on your device. Prompts and film roll metadata are never sent to third-party AI servers.
  • Astronomical solar azimuth, elevation, golden hour, and blue hour times are computed entirely offline using solar ephemeris formulas.

2. Exports & Purchases

ExifTool JSON, XMP sidecar, CSV, and PDF lab development sheets are generated locally on your device and shared only when you explicitly invoke the iOS Share Sheet. The optional one-time FrameSun Lifetime Pro purchase is processed solely by Apple via StoreKit 2.
