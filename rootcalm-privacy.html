RootCalm Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

RootCalm is built on an offline-first, on-device architecture. We do not operate developer servers that collect, track, or sell your houseplant records, rooms, soil moisture logs, or symptom triage notes. RootCalm contains zero ads, zero user accounts, and zero third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

1. On-Device Storage & Apple Intelligence

  • Your plant collection, room names, micro-climate settings, and care history events are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple SwiftData and local UserDefaults.
  • On eligible devices with Apple Intelligence enabled, RootCalm's Plant Triage Coach uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework (SystemLanguageModel.default) locally on your device. Plant symptoms and micro-climate context are never transmitted to third-party AI servers.

2. Exports & Purchases

Vacation Sitter CSV and PDF care sheets are generated locally on your device and shared only when you initiate a share via the iOS Share Sheet. The optional $3.99 one-time RootCalm Lifetime Pro purchase is processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2.

3. Contact

For privacy questions or support, email fremxhk@gmail.com.
