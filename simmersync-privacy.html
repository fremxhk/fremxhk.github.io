SimmerSync Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

SimmerSync is built on an offline-first, on-device architecture. We do not operate developer servers that collect, track, or sell your recipes, grocery lists, or photos. SimmerSync contains zero advertising and zero third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

1. On-Device Recipe Box & Vision OCR

  • Your saved recipes, cooking timelines, and grocery lists are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple SwiftData.
  • When you scan a cookbook page or recipe screenshot, Apple Vision OCR runs entirely on your device. Images are never uploaded to external servers or third-party AI APIs.

2. Exports & Sharing

Recipe PDF cards and grocery lists are generated locally on your device and shared only when you explicitly choose a destination via the iOS Share Sheet.

3. In-App Purchases

The optional $3.99 one-time SimmerSync Lifetime Pro unlock is processed exclusively by Apple through StoreKit 2. SimmerSync never receives or stores payment credentials.

4. Contact

For privacy questions or support, email fremxhk@gmail.com.
