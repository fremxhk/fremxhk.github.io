RotaFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

RotaFlow is designed with an offline-first, on-device architecture. We do not operate developer servers that collect, store, or sell your shift schedules, pay rates, or photos. RotaFlow contains zero ads and zero third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

1. On-Device Rota OCR & Schedule Storage

  • All shift calendars, job pay profiles, templates, and paycheck estimates are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple SwiftData.
  • When you import a rota photo or screenshot, Apple Vision OCR runs entirely on your device. Images are never uploaded to third-party servers or external AI APIs.

2. Optional Apple Calendar & .ics Sharing

If you choose to export shifts to Apple Calendar, RotaFlow requests write-only permission via Apple EventKit solely to create the shift events you select. Partner .ics calendar files are generated locally and shared only through the standard iOS Share Sheet when you initiate a share.

3. In-App Purchases

The optional $4.99 one-time RotaFlow Lifetime Pro unlock is processed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit 2. RotaFlow never sees or stores your payment details.

4. Non-Medical Schedule Notice

Turnaround and day-to-night flip notices in RotaFlow are general scheduling reminders only and do not provide medical, clinical, or occupational health advice.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or support, email fremxhk@gmail.com.
