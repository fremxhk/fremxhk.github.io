Enjoy Hong Kong-style sixteen-tile Mahjong on iPhone and iPad. Start an offline game with three computer opponents, or play with friends through Game Center and nearby connections.

A familiar table
Play in landscape with four players around the table. Discards stay in order at the center, while exposed melds and flowers remain visible. A newly drawn tile sits beside your hand; tap a legal tile to discard, and a retained draw sorts automatically.

Clear choices
See legal win, kong, pung and chow options with tile previews, or pass. Computer opponents decide from their own hand and public information.

Your house rules
Choose classic, advanced or casual scoring and adjust supported house rules and table themes. Optional tile exchange uses a shared die roll, player-selected packets and random redistribution.

Readable scoring
Review hand patterns, applicable dealer fan, base points, running debts and immediate flower awards. Completed game records are stored on your device.

Version 1.0 is free, with no ads or in-app purchases. Offline play needs no account or internet connection. Online play requires Apple Game Center. Scores have no cash value; there is no real-money betting, cash-out or valuable prize.

This app uses Hong Kong-style sixteen-tile Mahjong and configurable house rules. Please review the in-app rules before starting a table.

Game interface: Traditional Chinese.
