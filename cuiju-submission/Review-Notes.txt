2. Purpose and audience
Cuiju / 翠局 is a recreational Hong Kong-style sixteen-tile Mahjong game for Mahjong enthusiasts. Computer opponents, configurable house rules, legal-action prompts and detailed score receipts help players enjoy the game and understand scoring without keeping score manually. The app is intended for general public distribution and is not designed for children. Points have no monetary value, transfer, redemption or prizes; no real-money betting is provided.

3. Setup and main features
No developer account, login credentials, demo account or sample files are required for single-player. Launch and tap 單機雀局 to play with three computer opponents. The table uses landscape. Tap a legal tile to discard; 胡／槓／碰／上 appear when legal, and 過 declines an action. 公開牌面 displays exposed tiles. The receipt explains hand patterns, 莊 and 底, and settlement. 自訂雀例 opens configurable rules; the clock opens local history; settings opens themes, privacy and support.
There is no app-provided account registration/deletion, public posting/chat, advertising, purchasing or paid-content flow in this free release.

4. External services, tools and platforms
Apple GameKit/Game Center and MultipeerConnectivity provide optional Apple networking features. The Mahjong engine and computer opponents run locally; no external AI or game-data provider is used. SwiftData/UserDefaults store local records/settings. CloudKit game sync is disabled. GitHub Pages hosts the static policy/support pages, opened on request. Email is used only for voluntary support. No developer-operated game server, analytics/advertising SDK or payment processor is used.

5. Regions
The app has consistent features, content and player-selectable rules across enabled storefronts; no location-based rule changes. The app interface is Traditional Chinese; the English listing is not a claim of an English game UI. Age ratings and storefront availability reflect Apple/regional requirements. EU, mainland China, South Korea and Vietnam are excluded from this launch.

6. Regulated services and protected material
Recreational board/card game; no medical/financial or real-money gambling services, deposits, cash-out or valuable prizes. Mahjong/table artwork is drawn programmatically. The unmodified LXGW WenKai TC font is licensed under SIL OFL 1.1; the copyright and licence are bundled. No third-party game-data service or protected media catalogue is used.
