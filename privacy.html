Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile applications handle information. We are committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring transparency regarding data practices.

1. Data Collection & Processing

Our applications are designed with privacy as a foundational principle. We do not require account registration or collect personally identifiable information such as your name, residential address, phone number, or government identifiers.

Application features and data processing operate on-device to the fullest extent possible. Any user-generated content or personal settings remain securely stored within your device's local application sandbox.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development and maintenance, some of our free applications display third-party advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous device-level data to serve advertisements, prevent invalid traffic, and measure ad performance. This data may include:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location (city or country level).
  • Device identifiers (such as Apple's Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA, subject to your explicit consent).
  • Device model, operating system version, and general network connection type.
  • Aggregated ad performance metrics (e.g., ad views and interactions).

For more information on how Google manages advertising data, please review Google's Advertising Policies and Terms.

3. User Consent & Tracking Controls (Apple ATT & GDPR)

  • Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS, you will receive a prompt requesting permission to track. If you select "Ask App not to Track", your IDFA will not be accessed, and Google AdMob will display only contextual, non-personalized advertisements.
  • EEA, UK, & Switzerland (GDPR): For users in regions subject to European data privacy laws, a consent management message is presented via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to allow you to configure your consent preferences.

4. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe an app has inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect operational or legal changes. Updated versions will be published to this page with a revised date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at: fremxhk@gmail.com.
