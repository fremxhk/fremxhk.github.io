Muslin Delta Privacy Policy

Muslin Delta stores the inputs and named plans you enter on your device. The developer receives neither. The app includes no analytics, advertising, account registration, or third-party SDKs.

Lifetime Pro uses Apple's App Store billing. Apple handles the transaction and provides the app with the entitlement. If you choose Export, the system share sheet sends the text only to the destination you select.

You can remove saved work in the app's Saved work list. You can also delete the app and its local data from your device.

For privacy questions, email the developer.
