Daily Lingo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy describes how Daily Lingo for iPhone handles your information. Daily Lingo has no account, contains no advertising, does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools, and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. We do not operate a server that receives your information.

1. Information stored on your device

Daily Lingo stores your learning progress on your device: the languages you study, finished lessons, review schedules for words and sentences, streaks, daily goals, reminder settings and other preferences. The home screen widget reads a small summary of your streak and goal from storage shared between the app and the widget on the same device.

Saved expressions, conversation completion and recall records also form part of your learning progress. The app keeps local daily counts of onboarding, conversation starts/completions, paywall views and successful purchases in a rolling 90-day window; older counts are removed when you next open or use the app. These counts contain no answer text, conversation text, payment details or account identifiers. Settings offers an optional aggregate learning-summary export; nothing is transmitted automatically. If you choose to share an export, you select its destination using the system share sheet.

2. iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, your progress and purchase bookkeeping can sync between your own devices through your private iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit. A randomly generated installation identifier, combined with Apple's identifier for this app's vendor on the device, separates activity recorded on different installations so that their progress can be combined. It is used only for this sync bookkeeping, not advertising or tracking, and is stored with your private progress. Sync can take time; Settings shows account availability and observed transfers. This data is stored in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive it and cannot see, access or recover it.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking exercises use the microphone only while you practice. Your speech is recognized on your device with Apple's speech frameworks to compare it with the expected answer. Recordings are not saved and are not sent to us.

4. On-device translation and practice

The translation tool and the conversation practice features use Apple's on-device Translation and Apple Intelligence frameworks where your device supports them. Text you enter is processed on your device. Your device may download language models from Apple the first time you use a language.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks which purchases are active using Apple's StoreKit.

6. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Daily Lingo schedules local notifications on your device. They are not sent through any server of ours.

7. Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To remove synced progress from iCloud, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage, and delete the data for Daily Lingo. Your device's own backups may include app data according to your device settings.

8. Children's privacy

The app does not automatically collect personal information from anyone, including children. Please refer to the App Store listing for the current age rating.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised date. If a future version adds advertising, this page will describe it before that version is released.
