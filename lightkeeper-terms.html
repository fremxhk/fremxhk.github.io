Optional purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Localized prices and purchase conditions appear before confirmation. Oil is consumable virtual currency for in-game hints, has no cash value and cannot be transferred or redeemed for money. There are no paid random items. Puzzles can be solved without buying hints.

Keeper subscription

When offered, Keeper’s Lantern is an optional monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription. While active it grants access to the three cosmetic themes and double season XP for eligible first solves. The purchase screen provides the price, billing period and any eligible introductory offer. Apple determines trial eligibility. Payment is charged to your Apple account; subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Apple account settings. A free trial, if offered and eligible, converts to the displayed paid plan unless canceled.

Season content

A season pass applies to its named season and premium reward track. Check the dates and available rewards in the app before purchasing. Product availability and benefit descriptions must match the purchase screen.
