Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Please send an email to fremxhk@gmail.com with the app name, your device model, iOS/OS version, and a brief description of the issue or feature idea.

2. How are purchases and refunds handled?

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed directly through Apple App Store or Google Play. To request a refund or manage subscriptions, please visit reportaproblem.apple.com or your store account settings.
