How Does On-Device Rota OCR Import Work?

Open the Import tab and choose a screenshot or photo of your roster (or tap Try Sample Hospital Rota to preview). Apple Vision extracts dates, shift codes, and time ranges directly on your iPhone so you can review and import shifts in seconds.

Sharing Shifts with a Partner

Tap Share .ics on the Calendar tab to generate a standard .ics calendar file. Your partner can open it in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook without creating an account or installing another app.
