What is included Free vs Lifetime ($0.99)?

Free Forever: Unlimited iCloud-synced shared groups, automatic iCloud transfer to your next iPhone, 5 expense entries per local calendar day (across manual, receipt photo, or voice), Equal & Exact splits, Minimum-Cash-Flow debt simplification, and 30-day True Personal Spend summaries.

TallyShare Lifetime ($0.99 one-time, no subscription): Unlimited daily entries, Custom Credit Card FX Fee Profiles (% + fixed fee) & 1-tap statement total matching, optional daily FX rate updates, percentage/shares/multi-payer splits, monthly budgets, full Swift Charts Insights, CSV/PDF reports, and Face ID App Lock.

How does switching to a new iPhone work?

Sign into the same Apple Account on your new iPhone with iCloud Drive/CloudKit enabled for TallyShare. Both your Personal Ledger and Shared Groups restore automatically. You can also export an encrypted .tallysharebackup file anytime in Settings → iCloud Device Transfer & Backup.

Restoring Your Purchase

Open Settings inside TallyShare and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the Apple Account that completed the $0.99 Lifetime purchase.
